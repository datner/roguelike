# Code map

Generated by `scripts/map.sh`; do not edit. One line per module: path (size, rounded to 100 lines) — its `//!` summary. Signatures: `scripts/map.sh --sigs <file or dir>...` (nested = inside the `impl` or `mod` above; test modules skipped).

## game

- `game/src/ai.rs` (~900) — Non-player decisions. AI only chooses a `Command`; the scheduler queues it like any other action, so moving and bump-attacking reuse `TryMove`.
- `game/src/aim.rs` (~600) — Aiming, for frontends (ADR 0013): which tiles are worth targeting and what a shot at one would do, in plain data. Computed with the same functions the commands use, so the preview cannot disagree with the shot.
- `game/src/ammo.rs` (~400) — Ammunition handling (ADR 0013): which launcher is wielded, nocking an arrow, and the quiver. A nocked arrow is the only thing in its launcher's `Inventory`; a quiver's arrows are its `Inventory`, so both stay inert and are saved and despawned with their holder.
- `game/src/area.rs` (~200) — Which tiles a spell's `Shape` covers (ADR 0014): pure functions of the map, the caster's tile and the aimed tile, so a cast and its preview cover the same ground. Walls block. Tiles come nearest the centre first (ties by row, then column), which is also the order an area is animated in.
- `game/src/balance.rs` (~1000) — Balance bench (docs/plans/balance.md): a build set before training dolls for a window of ticks, through the real systems (`GameState`, commands, events), so what it reports is the game's, not a model's. The `bench` program drives it; nothing in play calls it.
- `game/src/balance/floors.rs` (~600) — The floor bot (docs/plans/floor-bot.md): a build played through real dungeon floors, from camp down the stairs, exploring and fighting what it meets with its policy until it dies or reaches the depth asked for. It is the naive player of the vision's Fights: it never retreats or rests on purpose, so what it reports is what bumping gets you.
- `game/src/blueprint.rs` (~1900) — Data-driven entity definitions, loaded from `data/entities/*.ron`. A blueprint's name is its file stem. Inheritance is resolved once at load time, so spawning never walks the parent chain.
- `game/src/camera.rs` (~100) — Which part of the zone a viewport shows (ADR 0009). The only place that converts between screen tiles and world tiles.
- `game/src/camp.rs` (~200) — Camp (docs/plans/dives.md): the safe place between dives, drawn in `data/camp.ron` as rows of characters. Each entrance is stairs down into a dungeon; a workbench is where things are made (docs/plans/crafting.md). Nothing hostile and nothing left there persists yet.
- `game/src/cell.rs` (~100) — The rendering seam's output (ADR 0001): one glyph and its colours per screen cell.
- `game/src/combat.rs` (~1700) — Combat events and handlers, plus the shared rolls used by both the real command applier and the side-effect-free `preview_damage`.
- `game/src/command.rs` (~1500) — `Command` and `apply_commands`: the only place the world is mutated during play (ADR 0002).
- `game/src/cost.rs` (~100) — Every action's energy cost, in one place so relative costs are reviewable. See ADR 0006 for the energy model.
- `game/src/craft.rs` (~1100) — Crafting (docs/plans/crafting.md): materials with properties, what creatures yield, and recipes over properties. Properties are listed in `data/properties.ron`, recipes in `data/recipes.ron`; a material names its own properties, and a product takes those of what went in.
- `game/src/discovery.rs` (~400) — Discovery (ADR 0018): combinations of skills that, once reached, inspire the learner with something new. Listed in `data/discoveries.ron`; checked only after a use with stakes, so practice never inspires.
- `game/src/dive.rs` (~1000) — Dives (docs/plans/dives.md): going down the stairs to a new floor. A floor exists only while you are on it: going down builds the next one from its own seed and forgets this one, keeping only you, what you hold, your effects and your summons.
- `game/src/dungeon.rs` (~400) — Dungeons (docs/plans/dives.md): `data/dungeons/<Name>.ron`, one per themed dungeon: what it is called, what you hear arriving, how its floors are generated and what lives on them. Loaded and checked with the blueprints, so a missing name fails at load, never on a floor.
- `game/src/effect.rs` (~500) — Timed effects (ADR 0006): entities attached to a target, carrying `Timed { start, duration }`. Whether an effect is active is always read from those timestamps; timers only perform end-of-effect side effects and periodic ticks, and each one re-checks the timestamps before acting.
- `game/src/entity.rs` (~1400) — Components: plain data stored in the hecs world. Behaviour lives in event handlers (see `event`) and in the command applier, never here.
- `game/src/equipment.rs` (~300) — Equipping items on body parts (Qud-style anatomy). An equipped item is in its owner's `Attachments` with `EquippedOn(part)`, so its parts take part in the owner's events; a carried one is in `Inventory` and inert.
- `game/src/event.rs` (~400) — Synchronous, typed event dispatch.
- `game/src/explore.rs` (~500) — Auto-explore: the player's next step toward an unfound floor item, then unexplored ground, then the stairs down, judged only from what the player knows.
- `game/src/fov.rs` (~300) — The player's field of view: Albert Ford's symmetric shadowcasting (ADR 0007). Symmetry — A sees B iff B sees A, between floor tiles — is what lets `ai::can_see_player` reuse the player's view instead of computing one per actor.
- `game/src/fx.rs` (~100) — Effects (ADR 0022): what just happened, for a frontend to animate: missiles in flight, damage dealt, misses and the like, deaths. The world has already moved on by the time they are read; nothing in the simulation reads them and they are never saved.
- `game/src/grammar.rs` (~200) — Log-message grammar: every sentence about an entity goes through a `Subject`, so the player reads "You take 3 damage." and everyone else "The goblin takes 3 damage."
- `game/src/health.rs` (~200) — Health that grows (docs/plans/sturdiness.md): max health is the blueprint's base plus what Toughness adds, and Toughness grows by being hurt in a fight. One place pays the skill off, as ADR 0017 asks. Sturdiness stays a composite: armor, resistances and avoidance are their own layers, grown elsewhere.
- `game/src/holding.rs` (~100) — Who holds what: the owner-side lists `Attachments` (in use, so its parts fire) and `Inventory` (carried, inert). Every change to either list goes through here, so how holding is stored can change in one place.
- `game/src/input.rs` (~200) — The rendering seam's input (ADR 0001): frontends translate raw keys into `InputEvent`.
- `game/src/legacy.rs` (~800) — Legacy (docs/plans/legacy.md, ADR 0026): what a life leaves when it ends, as a plain record the next characters of the same lineage are built from. Everything in it is a name, never an id, so content can change under it: a name that no longer exists is skipped, never an error.
- `game/src/lib.rs` (~100) — Simulation and game logic. This crate must never depend on a terminal/graphics crate (crossterm, ratatui, etc.) — that boundary is what makes the rendering seam real instead of aspirational.
- `game/src/light.rs` (~100) — Light as data: how strongly the player's held light reaches each tile in view, fading with distance. `render_view` bakes it into cell colours, as a terminal needs; a frontend that can blend draws it from `render_layers` instead.
- `game/src/lore.rs` (~400) — Lore (ADR 0017): what the player knows about the creatures they meet. Beast lore grows by novelty, the first sight of each kind of creature, and decides how much the look cursor reveals (`inspect`); Arcana grows by the first cast of each spell and reads the spells a creature knows.
- `game/src/map.rs` (~200) — Tiles, the generic `Grid<T>` (out-of-bounds reads are `None`), and spawn placement.
- `game/src/origin.rs` (~500) — Origins (docs/plans/origins.md, ADR 0025): what a character is when it starts, in place of a class. An origin names the blueprint the character is made from, which inherits `Player` and adds the kit, skills and techniques, and the styles it starts knowing. Listed in `data/origins.ron`; the first is the default.
- `game/src/phase.rs` (~600) — Phases (`docs/vision.md`, docs/plans/dives.md): the world turns through phases, and at camp you choose which one to wait for. A dive is fixed to the phase it began in; a dungeon's spawn entries may belong to some phases only. Listed in `data/phases.ron`; the first is where the world starts.
- `game/src/prio.rs` (~100) — Every event-handler priority in the game. Lower values run first. Keeping them in one place makes the relative ordering reviewable. Priorities must be unique per event type (`EventRegistry::on` panics otherwise); they may repeat across different events.
- `game/src/properties.rs` (~300) — Properties over generated play (docs/plans/content-free-tests.md): what the architecture promises, checked on many games instead of a few examples. A game is a seed and a list of `Act`s played through the real turn loop, so every state checked is one play can reach, and a failure shrinks to a short list.
- `game/src/ranged.rs` (~1000) — Flight of a thrown or fired thing (ADR 0013): the straight line it travels, the first creature on it, and where it comes to rest. No RNG: an attack always hits whoever is first in line.
- `game/src/rest.rs` (~400) — Rest (docs/plans/rest.md): health comes back a share at a time, from a budget per floor, so careful play can heal between fights but a floor is still a budget. A `Regeneration` holder's tick is a timer like bleeding's: it re-checks everything when it fires (ADR 0006). Camp heals in full. Recovery (docs/plans/sturdiness.md), trained by healing wounds taken in a fight, quickens the ticks and deepens the budget.
- `game/src/rng.rs` (~100) — Per-subsystem RNG streams (ADR 0005). Each subsystem draws only from its own `Pcg64` stream, so extra draws in one subsystem never shift another's results. Streams about a place (map, spawn) come from its zone seed (ADR 0009); the rest from the world seed.
- `game/src/rooms.rs` (~300) — Rooms and corridors: a floor of rectangular rooms, each joined by a short L-shaped corridor to the nearest room already joined, so every room is reachable. Built halls (a castle) beside the caves' grown ones (`zone::generate_cave`); a dungeon's data picks which (`dungeon::Generator`).
- `game/src/save.rs` (~1700) — Save games (ADR 0008): `GameState` to and from a RON string. File IO is the frontend's job; this module only converts.
- `game/src/side.rs` (~100) — Who fights whom (ADR 0015). Until there are factions there are two sides: hostile creatures and the player's. A summon is on its owner's side, so a goblin shaman's summons are hostile and the player's fight for them.
- `game/src/skill.rs` (~900) — Skills (ADR 0017): how well a creature does what it does, grown by doing it. Skills are flat, overlapping tags listed in `data/skills.ron`; one action uses several at once, and its level is their weighted average. Only XP is stored; levels are derived, so the curve can change freely.
- `game/src/spawn.rs` (~100) — `Spawn`: what to create and where it goes, handed to `GameState::spawn`, the only way entities are made (ADR 0008). Placement and holding are described here once instead of patched on after each spawn.
- `game/src/spell.rs` (~2400) — Spells (ADR 0014): what makes a cast possible and what it costs lives in the caster's disciplines, what it does lives in the spell. A cast fires `CheckCast` and `PayCost` at the caster, so a discipline is a part with handlers and `cast` never learns which resources exist.
- `game/src/sprint.rs` (~200) — Sprinting (docs/plans/fights.md): a toggle that makes a step cost half the energy and a point of stamina, and rules out attacking. Fleeing a foe as fast as you never works under the energy model; sprinting makes it a choice that runs out. A creature with `SprintAi` sprints while it chases from afar (the spirit wolf's charge).
- `game/src/state.rs` (~1300) — `GameState`, the message log, and `render_view`, which turns what the player knows into a `Grid<Cell>`.
- `game/src/style.rs` (~600) — Styles (ADR 0019): practices a creature discovers and follows. A style grows by uses that follow it; its principles switch on at levels of its skill, so a fresh style changes nothing. Known styles are carried and inert; the followed one is attached.
- `game/src/technique.rs` (~2800) — Techniques (ADR 0024): special actions done with the body and what it wields, such as a lunge. They share the spell's effect half (shapes, aiming) but not its payment: a technique costs a turn, a recharge and stamina, and its blow is an ordinary blow (`combat::blow`), so it lands, grazes and trains like a bump.
- `game/src/testing.rs` (~100) — Helpers for other crates' tests, behind the `testing` feature that only their dev-dependencies turn on: a frontend's flow test sets up what play would take many turns to reach. Never in play.
- `game/src/testkit.rs` (~200) — Test-only additions to `spawn::Spawn`. Tests describe an ad-hoc entity by what it is (`.item()`, `.armor(2)`, `.equipped_on(owner, 2)`), so a change to how a component or a holding is stored is made here once, not in every test.
- `game/src/tiles.rs` (~200) — Sprites (ADR 0023): `data/tiles.ron` names cells of an atlas image, one or more frames each, and which sprite each terrain uses; an `Appearance` names its sprite. Loaded and checked with the blueprints, so a missing name fails at load, never in play. Drawing them, and the clock that turns frames, is a frontend's.
- `game/src/time.rs` (~700) — Game time (ADR 0006): the global `Tick` clock, the timer queue, and the energy scheduler that decides who acts next.
- `game/src/training.rs` (~300) — Training on purpose (ADR 0017): lessons, a big chunk of XP once per kind of thing, and practice, a little XP a turn up to a ceiling. Neither has stakes, so neither repeats familiarity, trains a style or inspires.
- `game/src/view.rs` (~700) — Read-only views of the player's state for frontends: plain data, no hecs types, so frontends can draw panels without touching the world.
- `game/src/zone.rs` (~400) — Zones (ADR 0009): fixed-size areas larger than the screen, each seeded from the world seed and its coordinates, and the cellular-automata cave generator that fills them.

## front

- `front/src/crafting.rs` (~200) — Making things from the keyboard (docs/plans/crafting.md): the recipe list, a recipe's slots with a material picked by hand for any of them, and a batch run. Pure state; what a recipe needs, what fits and why it can't be made come from `game::recipe`, never worked out here.
- `front/src/input.rs` (~100) — What a frontend sends a `Session` or `Menu`, once its own key bindings have decided what a key means. Each mode reads one of these; `Mode::keys` says which.
- `front/src/lib.rs` (~100) — What every frontend shares: the keyboard flows (a `Session` and its modes), the title menu, and save files. No drawing and no key bindings, so a terminal and a window behave the same and only differ in how they look.
- `front/src/menu.rs` (~800) — Title menu: new game, continue, load, and save management (rename, delete). Pure state; `view` says what to draw.
- `front/src/overlay.rs` (~900) — The hovering panel's state: inventory, equipment, skills and styles tabs, the focused row, and picking an item for an empty body part. Pure state; a frontend draws it from the accessors and performs the actions `handle` returns.
- `front/src/saves.rs` (~500) — Save files: one per character, listed and managed from the title menu, written on quit.
- `front/src/session.rs` (~1000) — A game in progress as a frontend drives it: the state, which mode takes the keys (play, a list, a cursor), auto-explore and practice runs, and the status line. The rules of the keyboard flows live here once, so every frontend behaves the same; frontends only map keys and draw.
- `front/src/targeting.rs` (~500) — Throwing, casting and looking from the keyboard: a small list to pick what to throw or cast, then a cursor to pick where, and the look cursor. Pure state; what a shot would do comes from `game::aim`, what looking reveals from `game::inspect`, never worked out here.
- `front/src/text.rs` (~400) — Text the player reads in panels, as rows of segments a frontend styles: the character sheet, the equipment list, an item's name. Built once here so every frontend says the same thing.
- `front/src/title.rs` (~300) — Between games: performing the title menu's file actions, starting a character's game (their save, or a new one) and saving it on the way out. Frontends show the messages these return.

## gui

- `gui/src/app.rs` (~300) — The window: the title menu, then a game's map, side column and bottom strip. Key presses go to `front`'s `Menu` or `Session`; this module paces auto runs and draws.
- `gui/src/app/shots.rs` (~300) — Renders screens to PNGs for a human or an agent to look at, since nobody may run the window from here (`scripts/shots.sh`). Each shot is set up on its own, so any subset can be rendered, and cropped to the part of the window that changed: an image costs about width × height / 750 tokens to read.
- `gui/src/app/tests.rs` (~200) — (no module doc)
- `gui/src/fx.rs` (~300) — Effects on the map (ADR 0022), on the frame clock: a missile glides from tile to tile, damage and what a blow did rise from the struck tile, a struck tile flashes in reverse video and a dying one red, and the map shakes when the player is hurt. Glyphs and text on the cell grid, so they keep the terminal look. Any input finishes them all.
- `gui/src/input.rs` (~200) — Key bindings: the only place an egui key event becomes a `game::InputEvent` or a `front::input` value. Printable keys come from text events, so shifted symbols work on any layout.
- `gui/src/lib.rs` (~100) — Windowed frontend on eframe/egui (ADR 0020): maps keys to `front`'s inputs and draws, keyboard first, the mouse a shortcut onto the same flows. No game or flow rules here.
- `gui/src/main.rs` (~100) — Windowed frontend entry point: CLI args and wiring.
- `gui/src/map_view.rs` (~200) — Draws the game's `Grid<Cell>` into the map panel. The single tile↔pixel mapping on the `gui` side; which world tiles are shown is `game`'s camera.
- `gui/src/menu_view.rs` (~100) — Draws the title menu (`front::menu`): new game and its origin, continue, load, and save management, in a titled box.
- `gui/src/modes.rs` (~400) — Draws what the session's mode puts on and over the map: the look and aim cursors and marks, the throw and spell choosers, and the inventory panel. Pointer use on them becomes the same `front` inputs keys send, collected for `app` to hand to the session after drawing.
- `gui/src/panels.rs` (~100) — The panels around the map: the side column (character sheet, equipment) and the bottom strip (log and status), drawn from `front::text` and `Session::status` in the theme's titled boxes.
- `gui/src/sprites.rs` (~100) — Sprites (ADR 0023): the atlas `game::Tiles` names, as an egui texture, and the part of it a sprite shows at a moment. Off until F2, so glyphs stay the default look while the atlas is a placeholder.
- `gui/src/testing.rs` (~100) — Test helpers: a game set up from the real data, and the app in a headless window (`egui_kittest`), fed key and pointer events like eframe does.
- `gui/src/theme.rs` (~200) — The terminal look: one monospace font on a grid of text cells (`CELL`), black background, titled boxes with thin borders, the selected row in reverse video. Everything `gui` draws goes through these, so the window reads like a terminal.

## bench

- `bench/src/calibrate.rs` (~100) — Calibration (docs/plans/calibration.md): `playtime.csv`, written by the frontends beside the saves, read back as keypresses per hour, overall and per character, for the owner to set `actions_per_hour` in `bench/targets.ron` by hand.
- `bench/src/floors.rs` (~500) — The floor bot's half of the bench (docs/plans/floor-bot.md): the dives in `bench/dives.ron`, each listed build played through each over the seeds by `game::balance::dive`, and the report's Floors section: what a floor costs and gives, and how dives end.
- `bench/src/jobs.rs` (~100) — Running the bench's games on every core (docs/plans/bench-cli.md): each run is a game of its own, built from its seed, so they share nothing and the order they finish in changes no number. Results come back in job order, so the report reads the same as a run on one core. While a terminal watches, one stderr line says how far along it is.
- `bench/src/lib.rs` (~700) — Balance bench (docs/plans/balance.md): loads the builds and scenarios in `bench/`, runs each build against each scenario through `game::balance` over many seeds, and writes the report. A tool for changing content, not part of the game.
- `bench/src/main.rs` (~100) — Balance bench entry point, run as `cargo balance` (docs/plans/bench-cli.md). With no mode it runs everything and rewrites `bench/report.md` and `bench/report.json`, then says what moved; `--check` compares instead and fails if anything moved; `--print` prints; `--page FILE` writes the readable page (`bench/page.html` filled in, with what moved since the committed JSON); `--calibrate [FILE]` reads a `playtime.csv` (the gui's, by default) and prints keypresses an hour. `--seeds N` sets the doll and the dive seeds alike; `--build`, `--scenario`, `--dive` (each repeatable), `--dolls` and `--dives` narrow a run. A run unlike the committed one (narrowed, or other seeds) only prints.
- `bench/src/moved.rs` (~100) — What moved between two reports (docs/plans/bench-cli.md): their rows matched by section and name, so a change reads as one row's before and after, not as a diff's removed and added lines.
- `bench/src/targets.rs` (~300) — Pacing targets (`docs/vision.md`, Pacing; docs/plans/floor-bot.md): the ranges in `bench/targets.ron`, the floors a build takes to reach a level, and which measured numbers fall outside their range. A moved target is a design change, committed with its reason.

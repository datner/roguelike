# Plan: the floor bot

Handoff document. Read `docs/vision.md` (Fights, Pacing), `docs/plans/balance.md` (the bench as it is),
ADR 0005 (determinism), ADR 0009 (zones), ADR 0017 (skills) and `game/CLAUDE.md` first.

## Goal

The bench measures a build against dolls that never fight back. The vision's pacing targets (a floor
in 150–300 actions, a normal fight in 3–8, the first style principle within 1–2 dives) are about real
floors. The **floor bot** plays a build through real dungeon floors: from camp, down the stairs, auto-
exploring and fighting what it meets with the build's policy, floor after floor, until it dies or
reaches the depth asked for. Over many seeds it reports what a floor costs and yields, and how far
the targets are from what the game does. It is the naive player of Fights: it never retreats, kites or
heals on purpose, so it reads as "what bumping gets you".

## Decisions taken for this plan

- **Same home as the doll bench.** The simulation is in `game/src/balance.rs` (a `dive` beside `run`),
  the program in `bench`. It drives the player through `take_turn`/`time::submit` and the real
  `explore_step`, `travel_step` and `Descend`, like a keyboard, and measures by reading the world and
  `fx`; no counter is added to the game (the balance.rs rule).
- **A real game, not an arena.** A run is `GameState::new_as(seed, blueprints, origin)`, then the
  build's dress (skills, stats, gear, style) as the doll bench does it, except that **the build keeps
  `Learner`**: XP per floor is the point. It starts in camp, walks to the named dungeon's entrance
  (`Camp::entrance_at`) with `travel_step`, waits for the named phase if asked (`WaitForPhase`, in camp
  as a player would), and descends.
- **The bot's turn**, re-decided every turn, in order:
  1. An enemy in view (`side::enemies`, through the player's view, ADR 0007): the build's policy at the
     nearest one, as against a doll (`command`), generalized from dolls to enemies. Nothing in the
     policy applies: wait.
  2. Otherwise `explore_step`, with `ExploreItems::PickUp`, so it gathers what it passes (it never
     equips or uses it: that is smart play).
  3. Explore has nowhere to go and the bot stands on the stairs down: descend. Anywhere else: the
     floor is **stuck**.
- **Limits so a run always ends**: a floor ends as stuck after 2000 player turns or 50 waits in a row
  (an enemy in view it cannot reach). A stuck floor ends the run and is counted, not hidden.
- **Two counts of actions.** **Turns**: every action the player takes. **Keypresses**: what a person
  would press, the unit of the Pacing targets: a run of explore steps is one, every fight action is
  one, a descent is one, pickups during explore are free.
- **What a floor reports** (`FloorRun`): turns, keypresses, ticks, XP gained (summed over skills), the
  skill that gained most, HP lost and HP left, whether the bot died there (and to what, from the log's
  last blow), stuck or not, and each **fight's length** in keypresses (a fight runs from an enemy
  coming into view to none being in view).
- **The dive scenarios are data**, in `bench/dives.ron`: which builds (by name, from `bench/builds/`),
  which dungeons and phases, how many floors (6 by default: two way-landings' worth, since no dungeon
  has a bottom yet) and seeds (20 by default; floors are slower than dolls). Builds keep their policy;
  the naive builds are the bump-only ones.
- **Targets are data too**, in `bench/targets.ron`: the vision's Pacing table as ranges (fight length,
  keypresses per floor, floors to style level 5, …) and `actions_per_hour: 1000`. The report marks a
  measured number outside its range. A moved target is a design change, committed with the reason.
- **Progression by arithmetic, extrapolation marked.** From mean XP per floor for the build's
  most-trained skill and the skill curve (`skill::xp_for`), the bench works out the floors to levels 5,
  10 and 15. Beyond the floors actually played it extrapolates from the last floor's rate and says so.
- **In the checked-in report.** A **Floors** section in `bench/report.md` and its JSON, regenerated by
  `scripts/balance.sh` and diffed by `--check`. A moved number there is a balance change, like any other.

## Slices

Each slice ends with `scripts/check.sh` green and a commit, and is marked done here first.

### 1. One bot, one dive
- `balance.rs`: `Dive { name, dungeon, phase: Option<String>, floors }`, `dive(blueprints, &Build,
  &Dive, seed) -> Result<DiveRun, String>`, `DiveRun { floors: Vec<FloorRun>, ended: End }` with `End`
  `Depth | Died | Stuck`. The policy's target becomes "the nearest enemy in view" (dolls are enemies,
  so `run` keeps working through the same function).
- The camp walk, the phase wait, the bot's turn and the limits, as above.
- Tests (in `game`, on a small test dungeon from inline blueprints, not `data/`): a dive is the same
  per seed; an unarmed bot on a floor with no enemies reaches the floor asked for; a bot meets a goblin
  and the fight is counted; a bot that dies ends the run with `Died` on that floor; a floor the bot
  can't finish ends `Stuck`; the build gains XP (unlike `run`).
- Measure how long a dive takes; set the default seeds so `scripts/balance.sh` stays under two minutes,
  and note the time here.

### 2. Into the bench
- `bench/dives.ron`; `bench` loads it and runs every listed build × dungeon × phase over the seeds.
- Report **Floors** section, one table per dive scenario: per floor, how many runs are still alive,
  keypresses and turns (mean, p10, p90), XP, HP lost, fight length; how runs ended (depth, died with the
  commonest causes, stuck).
- JSON gains the same numbers and the per-seed spread.
- First scenarios: each origin as it starts (the naive bump build) in the caves and the castle, in the
  starting phase and the Embers; the level-10 builds in the caves.
- Tests: `bench` formats a fixed `DiveRun` into the expected table, as `report` is pinned today.

### 3. Targets and progression
- `bench/targets.ron` (the Pacing table as ranges, `actions_per_hour`), loaded with the rest.
- Floors to levels 5, 10 and 15 per scenario, extrapolation marked; keypresses converted to minutes.
- The report marks every number outside its target range, and lists them in a short **Off target**
  block at the top of the Floors section.
- `docs/vision.md` Pacing points at `bench/targets.ron` as the place the targets live.
- Tests: the floors-to-level arithmetic (a fixed XP per floor gives the expected floors, beyond-played
  marked); a number outside its range is marked, one inside is not.

### 4. On the page
- `bench/page.html`: a survival curve per scenario (runs alive by floor), keypresses per floor against
  the target band, fight lengths against theirs, and the Off target block.
- `/balance-report` skill: its reading covers the Floors section and the targets.
- Tests: the page builds from a fixed report with a Floors section.

## Out of scope

- Smarter play: retreating, kiting, resting on purpose, equipping finds, using consumables, choosing
  fights. The bot is naive on purpose; a smarter bot is a later plan, once Fights' answers exist.
- Rest budget, way-landings, a dungeon's bottom, new creatures: their own plans. The bot picks them up
  as content: resting once there is a rest action, landings once floors have them.
- Lives, endings and mentors: design-review targets until legacy exists.
- Recording real play time in `front` (the calibration in Pacing): its own small plan.
- Tuning: the bench reports, the owner decides.
- Running in CI or `check.sh`.
